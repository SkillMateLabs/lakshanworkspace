❓ How to play

  • Move: WASD / arrows, Jump: Space, Look: mouse (click game to lock). Stuck? Press U to pop free!
  • Fly 🕊️: G, double-tap Space, or the 🕊️ Fly button. Hold Space to rise, Shift (or C) to sink. On phone: tap 🕊️, then ⤒ up / ⤓ down.
  • See yourself 🧍: you play as a human — press V to switch between seeing yourself and first-person view. Everyone in the valley is human like you! New clothes from shops change your shirt, hats sit on your head!
  • Break: left click · Place: right click · Pick block: 1–9 or scroll or tap hotbar
  • Out of blocks or energy? Press Q, pick Math / Science / English / Music / 3D Shapes, answer!
  • 3D Shapes mission: place a Cube (6 square faces) and a Pyramid (4 triangle faces + 1 square base) and read their fact cards.
  • Cozy: E interact (pick flower / talk / shop / rest), I bag, B ride bike, H bell, T chat with friends, Esc closes panels.
  • Earn petals 🪷: pick wildflowers, shells, mushrooms & starflowers (E), sell at Flower Shop / Season Market. Spend in 11 shops + 1 seasonal pop-up.
  • Dig deep ⛏️: the earth has layers — grass, dirt, stone, then a RED bedrock level. Buy the ⚒️ Drill Shovel at Gadget Tool Shed (far north-east) to drill it for bonus energy + knowledge! Volt Electronics (far north-west) sells lamps, radios & energy batteries.
  • Mining Fields ⛏️: breaking blocks works ONLY in the Mining Fields — press ⛏️ Mine (or M) to jump there and dig freely, press again when done to come back out. Town is for building (right-click places), quizzes, shops & friends.
  • Seasons 🌸☀️🍁❄️: drift every 5 min (or click the season pill). Stock, open shops & pickups change. Flower Shop rests autumn/winter, Bike Shop rests winter, Teahouse rests summer.
  • Home 🏡: you wake up INSIDE your own BIG cottage room (solid roof overhead, wide door). E on the bed naps, E on the door mat steps out to your village lot — every player's house is in a different spot, and yours is labeled 🏡 MY HOUSE with a golden beacon. Decorate inside + outside with furniture & blocks!
  • Trees 🌳: trees never grow on top of you, spawn, or doorways anymore — walk around trunks, chop them if one blocks you, and press U if you ever get wedged.
  • Open world 🌍: no walls, no edges — walk or fly as far as you like, the meadow keeps growing under your feet. Flowers & trees keep appearing out there too. The minimap now follows you.
  • Friends 👥: press F or the Friends button for live multiplayer (falls back to cozy bot neighbours). Talk button / T chats. Note: while the mouse is captured for looking, press Esc first to click HUD buttons — or just use the letter keys.
  • Phone: left stick moves, drag right side to look, buttons to jump / mine / build.
